Prommt – AI Video Contest Platform 🎥🤖

Prommt is an AI-powered video contest platform designed to make creating, managing, and participating in video contests simple, fast, and engaging. The app is available on both Android and iOS, delivering a modern and seamless user experience.

🚀 Features

🎯 Create and manage video contests easily

🤖 AI-powered video handling and contest workflows

📤 Upload and participate in contests with ease

🏆 Transparent and engaging contest experience

📱 Modern, user-friendly UI

⚡ Fast and scalable performance

🛠 Tech Stack

Framework: Flutter

Platforms: Android & iOS

Architecture: Scalable and modular

AI Integration: Used for enhanced video contest experience

🎯 Purpose

Prommt aims to simplify video-based competitions by leveraging AI and modern mobile technologies, making it easy for organizers to host contests and for users to participate effortlessly.
